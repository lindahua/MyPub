# Publication locators

This document specifies the publication URL requirements for MyPub catalog records. [SCHEMAS.md](SCHEMAS.md) defines the JSON field forms and catalog-wide constraints; [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) defines application workflows. These rules apply to the optional `official_url` and `paper_url` fields and the required `extra_urls` array in schema version 2.

## General requirements

`official_url` is the official landing page for the specific publication and version, showing its title, authors, and abstract. It must not lead to a related version, generic venue page, project page, or file download. `paper_url` is a direct link to that publication's paper file, normally a PDF; it may redirect to the file. A verified publisher eReader URL that displays the exact paper and exposes its PDF download is also acceptable where specified below. `extra_urls` holds other useful publication resources, including former landing pages and DOI resolver links. All three fields contain remote absolute URIs, not local attachment paths. `official_url` and `paper_url` are independently optional; `extra_urls` is a required array of unique URIs.

When the official page identifies a DOI, verify it is the publication record's DOI. MyPub checks DOIs encoded in recognized official URL paths locally; DOI metadata shown only in page contents requires source verification. If the same official page is attached to multiple records, resolve their identity before assigning its DOI to another record.

Omit unknown `official_url` and `paper_url` values. Do not classify an arbitrary existing URL by array position or assume a URL is a direct paper link just because it contains “pdf”; verify its target or use a trusted provider’s explicit link. A direct file URL need not end in `.pdf`. These remote links do not download or register managed attachments. For arXiv, normally use `https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>` and `https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>` for the latest version. Use version-specific `/abs/<id>v<version>` and `/pdf/<id>v<version>` links when a record represents an earlier version with materially different title or content; verify that version against the record. Native JSON and CSV preserve both named fields; BibTeX exports the official page as `url` and also uses explicit `official_url`/`paper_url` extension fields to preserve their roles. An unclassified imported BibTeX/CSV `url` remains in `extra_urls`. Missing source values never erase existing named links. URL format is validated locally; page contents and file availability require source verification.

A journal or conference `paper_url` must point to that publication version’s own paper file, not to an arXiv preprint PDF. Once a publication paper URL is available, remove preprint links from `official_url` and `extra_urls` as part of the same edit; do not move the former arXiv link into `extra_urls`. Existing journal and conference records whose main `paper_url` still points to arXiv remain pending source correction. A workshop record may use an arXiv PDF when it is the available file for that workshop paper. A preprint may use its own arXiv PDF. Keep a verified older `official_url` when the preferred publisher page is unavailable; do not fabricate a destination from a DOI or identifier. The audit and registration constraints for paper files are defined in [SCHEMAS.md section 16](SCHEMAS.md#16-whole-catalog-validation) and the download workflow in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Venue-specific official pages

For CVPR and ICCV proceedings records, including workshop papers, `official_url` is the corresponding IEEE Xplore document page (`https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/<number>`). Verify the page against the publication title, venue, and year before assigning it. Preserve other useful landing pages, such as the CVF Open Access and IEEE Computer Society Digital Library pages, in `extra_urls`. If an Xplore article page is not yet verifiable, retain the existing `official_url` until one is available. For `paper_url`, prefer the PDF link on the exact matching CVF Open Access paper page when one exists. Verify the CVF title, year, and proceedings track before using its link; retain a verified existing paper file link when CVF does not host that edition or paper. After correcting a `paper_url`, re-download and register the file from that URL.

For ECCV conference and workshop records, first look for the exact paper on an ECVA proceedings or conference paper page and use its direct PDF link as `paper_url` when available. The PDF may be hosted on a conference media domain linked from that page; use the page's actual link. Verify the title, authors, year, and track on ECVA and the downloaded PDF before assigning the link. For earlier editions or papers without an ECVA PDF, use the direct PDF link from the matching Springer Nature Link chapter when available; verify the chapter and PDF against the publication. Keep a verified existing paper file link when neither source provides a confirmed PDF. After correcting a `paper_url`, re-download and register the file from that URL. For ECCV conference records, `official_url` is the matching Springer Nature Link chapter page (`https://link.springer.com/chapter/<DOI>`) when available. Verify the chapter title, ECCV edition, year, and author against the record. Keep ECVA and other previous landing pages in `extra_urls`. A verified ECVA publication page may serve as `official_url` while the Springer chapter page is unavailable; track its replacement in a shared To-Do item.

For ICLR conference records from 2024 onward, `official_url` is the corresponding ICLR proceedings paper page (`https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/<year>/hash/<hash>-Abstract-Conference.html`). Verify its title, year, and authors. Set `paper_url` to that page's exact **Paper** PDF link on `proceedings.iclr.cc`, then download, verify, and register that proceedings PDF as the primary attachment. Store the separately verified OpenReview forum ID in `identifiers.openreview`; it does not replace the proceedings `official_url`. For earlier editions not hosted on the proceedings site, use the paper's official OpenReview forum page (`https://openreview.net/forum?id=<id>`) as `official_url`, store the matching forum ID, and use that page's **Download PDF** link (`https://openreview.net/pdf?id=<id>`) as `paper_url`. Download, verify, and register the OpenReview PDF. The audit warns when an active ICLR paper URL uses the wrong source for its edition. Preserve previous conference or project landing pages in `extra_urls`, but remove arXiv preprint links when assigning the conference paper URL. ICLR's yearly proceedings ISBN identifies the volume, not an individual paper.

For ICML main-conference records, `official_url` is the matching PMLR paper page (`https://proceedings.mlr.press/v<volume>/<paper>.html`) when published. Verify the page's title, ICML edition, year, and authors. Set `paper_url` to that exact page's **Download PDF** link after verifying the downloaded paper. The link may be hosted by PMLR or by PMLR's `mlresearch` GitHub repository; follow the page's link rather than constructing a PDF URL from the paper slug. After correcting a `paper_url`, re-download and register that PDF as the primary paper attachment. Keep prior ICML conference pages in `extra_urls` when replacing them. Until the PMLR volume is available, a verified ICML conference page may serve as `official_url`; use the matching ICML OpenReview forum's **Download PDF** link (`https://openreview.net/pdf?id=<forum-id>`) as `paper_url` and register that PDF. Track replacement of both URLs and the managed PDF with PMLR sources in a shared To-Do item. ICML main-conference papers may lack an individual DOI; do not assign a DOI from another publication or version. If the official page supplies a DOI, store it and apply the normal DOI uniqueness and URL consistency checks. ICML workshop records use the publication page for their own workshop proceedings and are not mapped to the main-conference volume.

For IEEE Transactions journal records, `official_url` is the matching IEEE Xplore document page (`https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/<number>`). Verify the DOI against the article title and Transactions journal, then resolve it to the Xplore document number. Preserve former DOI resolver and other landing pages in `extra_urls`; keep the DOI identifier when present.

For International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV) records, `official_url` is the matching Springer Nature Link article page (`https://link.springer.com/article/<DOI>`). Verify the article title, journal, and DOI before assigning it. Use that page's **Download PDF** link (`https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/<DOI>.pdf`) as `paper_url` when accessible. Verify the downloaded PDF's title and DOI, then register it as the primary paper attachment and remove any former arXiv preprint link. If Springer requires a subscription unit or other paid access, leave the record pending until access is authorized. The audit warns when an active IJCV paper URL is missing or does not match its DOI on Springer. Preserve former DOI resolver links in `extra_urls` and keep the DOI identifier.

For ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG) records, `official_url` is the matching ACM Digital Library article page (`https://dl.acm.org/doi/<DOI>`). Verify the article title, journal, and DOI before assigning it. Preserve former DOI resolver links in `extra_urls` and keep the DOI identifier.

For SIGGRAPH conference records, including SIGGRAPH Asia and posters, `official_url` is the matching ACM Digital Library publication page (`https://dl.acm.org/doi/<DOI>`). Verify the title, venue, year, and DOI before assigning it. Set `paper_url` to that page's **View PDF** link (`https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/<DOI>`). Its eReader **Download PDF** link may add `?download=true`; use that link to obtain the publisher PDF, verify its title and DOI, and register it as the primary paper attachment. Remove any former arXiv preprint link. The audit warns when an active SIGGRAPH record's paper URL does not match its ACM DOI. Preserve former DOI resolver links in `extra_urls` and keep the DOI identifier.

For ACM Multimedia (ACM MM) conference records, `official_url` is the matching ACM Digital Library paper page (`https://dl.acm.org/doi/<DOI>`). Verify the title, venue, year, and DOI before assigning it. Use the matching ACM PDF/eReader page (`https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/<DOI>`) as `paper_url` when it displays the paper and exposes a Download PDF link. Download and register that ACM PDF as the primary paper attachment; record the actual ACM download link as the attachment `source_url`. Remove any former arXiv link from the conference record rather than moving it to `extra_urls`. Preserve former DOI resolver links in `extra_urls` and keep the DOI identifier.

For other active publications whose `official_url` is an ACM Digital Library DOI page, use that page's **View PDF** link (`https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/<DOI>`) as `paper_url`. Its eReader may provide a **Download PDF** link with `?download=true`; use the page's actual download link to obtain the publisher file. Verify the PDF title and DOI, register it as the primary attachment, and record the actual download link as the attachment `source_url`. Remove any former arXiv preprint link. The audit warns when an ACM publication's paper URL is missing or does not point to the matching ACM PDF or eReader page.

For Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS) records, `official_url` is the matching ACS Publications article page on `pubs.acs.org`. Verify the article title, journal, and DOI before assigning it. Preserve former DOI resolver links in `extra_urls` and keep the DOI identifier.

For Nature Nanotechnology records, `official_url` is the matching Nature article page (`https://www.nature.com/articles/<article-id>`). Verify the article identity and DOI, accounting for author-manuscript title variants. Preserve former DOI resolver links in `extra_urls` and keep existing DOI identifiers.
