# DOI audit — 2026-09-26

The catalog contains **832** publication records. **740** have unique stored DOIs; **32 active ICLR** papers have unique OpenReview forum IDs in place of paper DOIs; **55 other active** records lacked a DOI at the time of the initial audit. Under the later ICML exception, 13 active ICML main-conference records require no missing-DOI warning, leaving 42 active missing-DOI warnings. Five archived records lack a DOI and are excluded from missing-identifier audit warnings. No duplicate stored DOI or contradiction with a DOI encoded in a recognized official URL was found after the updates.

This audit added 433 arXiv-issued DOIs where the official arXiv URL and stored arXiv ID agree, 13 ACL Anthology DOIs where official page title and DOI metadata agree, and 5 Crossref candidates independently confirmed on official IJCAI or Robotics: Science and Systems pages. arXiv's [DOI policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/doi.html) gives the arXiv DOI form. Crossref's [REST API](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/) supplied additional search candidates.

ICLR is tracked separately: the [official proceedings FAQ](https://iclr.cc/FAQ/Proceedings) lists yearly proceedings ISBNs and OpenReview access, rather than individual paper DOIs. The 32 active ICLR records now store distinct case-sensitive `identifiers.openreview` forum IDs. For 29 records, exact-title OpenReview API results matched the ICLR conference year and first author; three IDs came from existing official OpenReview forum URLs. Existing ICLR Proceedings paper pages remain the official URLs where available.

An online check of all 307 non-preprint records with stored DOIs found DOI metadata on 104 official pages; all 104 agreed. The other 203 pages did not expose a DOI to the metadata parser (many returned HTTP 202 or 403), so this is not a complete live-page verification. The read-only script is `node scripts/doi-page-audit.mjs CATALOG_ROOT --with-doi`. Crossref was queried for all 103 then-unresolved non-preprint records at its public one-request-per-second limit; five candidates passed title, author, year, venue, uniqueness, and official-page checks. The read-only lookup is `node scripts/doi-crossref-audit.mjs CATALOG_ROOT`.

At the time of the initial audit, of the 55 active records outside ICLR that lacked a DOI, 2 have no official URL, 52 did not yield a unique DOI in the fetched page metadata, and 1 needs manual venue/version review. A follow-up removed 12 archived copies confirmed to duplicate active publications and moved three publisher DOIs from archived copies to their active counterparts; earlier review evidence remains in Git. These categories describe verification status, not proof that a DOI does not exist. Do not assign the DOI of a different publication version merely to fill a missing value.
